About Richard

Ideas first.
Technology in service of story.

Richard Feng creates cinematic short-form advertising for real estate, legal services, medical aesthetics and local businesses. His work combines concept development, scripting, AI-assisted production, editing and sound into one focused creative process.

Known to Chinese-speaking audiences as 小疯未眠 and in English as Mr.Feng No Sleep, Richard uses one creative identity across commercial video and original music. The common thread is a focus on rhythm, emotion and memorable visual storytelling.

Rather than treating AI as the idea, Richard uses it as a production tool. Each project starts with the audience, the offer and the story the brand needs to tell.

Richard 的创作从受众、商业目标与故事出发，再以 AI 制作工具实现画面。他希望帮助品牌用更有人情味、更具电影感、也更值得观看的方式表达自己。
